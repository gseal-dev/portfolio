Inventory Management

Modern, responsive Django-based inventory and retail management platform designed to help businesses manage their products, sales, purchases, and overall operations in one place.

Screenshots

Key Features

Core Management

  • Dashboard: Real-time business insights, stock alerts, and quick performance metrics.
  • Product & Category Manager: Full CRUD operations for products and categories, including automatic SKU generation and custom barcode generation/exporting.
  • Notifications: Built-in system alerts for low-stock products and operational events.

Transactional Workflows

  • POS (Point of Sale): Interactive, lightweight register for processing cash and bank sales.
  • Sales & Purchases: Detailed logging of transactions with automatic stock updates.
  • Stock Transfer: Manage and track inventory movements across different warehouses.
  • Financial Tracking: Logging and monitoring for business expenses, cash, and bank accounts.

Administration & Settings

  • Warehouse Management: Track stock distribution across locations.
  • Parties: Manage supplier and customer profiles.
  • Staff Management: Keep records of team members and roles.
  • System Settings & Personalization: Dynamic store naming, logo uploads, local currency/date format customization, and a UI theme color picker.

Tech Stack

Backend

Python, Django 6.0

Database

SQLite (default)

Frontend

Responsive HTML5, Vanilla CSS, Inter Typography

Dependencies

python-barcode (barcode generation), pillow (image handling)
